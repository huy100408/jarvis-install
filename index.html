01 · Voice and text

Talk to it like a person.

Push to talk or type. JARVIS answers in natural speech, searches the web when it needs to, and keeps every chat in the sidebar.

02 · iPhone companion

Your Mac screen, in your pocket.

Watch the live screen from your iPhone or iPad over LAN or Tailscale. Tap and type on it directly, or hand JARVIS a task from the couch.

03 · Autonomous agent

It looks, acts, then checks its work.

Each step reads the screen and accessibility tree, makes one move with the mouse, keyboard, shell or AppleScript, and verifies the result before the next.

Nothing sensitive happens without you.

Passwords, payments, keychain access and destructive commands pause the agent and ask your iPhone first. Secrets you type with Secure Input go straight to the field, never to the model or the logs.

Uninstall

Removes Jarvis completely: the app, background services, the jarvis command, logs, settings, sign-in and saved keys. It lists everything first and asks before removing anything. Add --keep-data (Mac) or -KeepData (Windows) to keep your settings for a later reinstall. Python and Homebrew stay installed.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

What is JARVIS?+

JARVIS is an autonomous AI desktop assistant designed for macOS and Windows. It combines screen vision, mouse and keyboard automation, local terminal tools, and natural voice conversations into a single desktop application.

How does JARVIS safely execute computer actions?+

JARVIS incorporates a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Security Gatekeeper. Any destructive shell command, payment, keychain access, or credential request automatically pauses execution and routes an approval prompt to your iPhone or iPad before proceeding.

How do I install JARVIS on Mac and Windows?+

On macOS (Apple Silicon M-series), download the Jarvis.dmg installer or paste curl -fsSL https://huy100408.github.io/jarvis-install/install.sh | bash into Terminal. On Windows 10 and 11, run Jarvis-Setup.exe or execute irm https://huy100408.github.io/jarvis-install/install.ps1 | iex in PowerShell.

Does JARVIS require an API key to get started?+

No. JARVIS includes managed cloud AI access upon signing in with Google (5 free messages per day / 30 per month). You can also link your own custom AI provider keys (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Antigravity AI) in Settings at any time.

Can I control my desktop from my iPhone?+

Yes. You can sideload the JARVIS iOS app (Jarvis.ipa) to view the live desktop screen at 24 fps, dispatch voice commands, approve security prompts, and operate your computer remotely over LAN or Tailscale.
